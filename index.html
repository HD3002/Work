🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to create findings, generate evidence and export directly into Jira.

1

Configure Your Test Session

Enter the ticket number and select the Platform, Environment and Browser you are validating against.

2

Add Findings

Enter the Site Section being tested and provide a clear comment describing the outcome of the validation.

3

Upload Screenshots

Attach screenshots as evidence. The images will automatically appear in the Evidence Preview section.

4

Review Evidence

Confirm all screenshots, comments and configuration details before exporting.

5

Copy Into Jira

Click "Copy For Jira" and paste directly into your Jira comment using Ctrl + V.

6

Export Evidence

Export findings to Excel for future reference, auditing or stakeholder review.

💡 Best Practice Tips

  • Use PNG screenshots where possible for maximum clarity.
  • Use comments beginning with "As a user..." for consistency.
  • Verify screenshots before exporting evidence packs.
  • Save templates for recurring validation activities.
  • Configure visible columns before exporting to Excel.
  • Use Copy For Jira whenever possible to reduce manual effort.
